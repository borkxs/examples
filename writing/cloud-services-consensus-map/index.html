Cloud Services Consensus Map

A filterable map of cloud services, the distributed-systems ideas they resemble or publicly cite, and the papers worth reading next.

The point

Cloud product pages describe managed databases, queues, storage, and edge runtimes as separate categories. Underneath, many of them rhyme: replicated logs, quorum writes, leader election, metadata stores, clocks, anti-entropy, and conflict resolution.

This map is a reading guide, not an audit of private implementations. Treat each card as a pointer from a product shape to the research vocabulary that helps explain it.

Takeaway
